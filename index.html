About Me

Petroleum & Gas - Power - Petrochemical Industry

Significant experience gained in the welding and fabrication industry. Working Offshore/Onshore Power stations, petrochemical plants, and oil refineries at fabrication yards on green field sites and live installations.

Welding Opr. / Inspector / Supervisor / Trainer (33 Years), taking full responsibility for Production welds made on sites. Expert in deployment of welding crews and compliance with approved Welding Procedures and protocols. Overseeing and ensuring the quality of workmanship as per client specifications.

Proficient in calling up intermediate and final N.D.E, carrying out welder qualification tests using approved welding procedures in adherence to client specifications in conjunction with ASME IX / B31.3 / EN 287-288 / API 1104 / BS 4515.

Technical Skills

  • Experience gained in power plants, refineries, oil & gas processes plants, on-shore gas & oil pipelines, process piping, low pressure & storage tanks, and heavy steel structures.
  • Deep experience in fabrication and erection of piping, boilers, pressure vessels, furnaces, tanks, heaters, and structural steels.
  • Expert skills in preparation of spooling, weld map, pressure test packs, piping & weld log books, and fabrication/erection progress follow-up records.
  • Sound experience with International codes and standards: ISO, DIN, EN, BS, ASME, ASTM, ANSI, ASNT & API.
  • Hands-on experience with SMAW, GTAW, GMAW / GS-FCAW, SAW, and fusion cutting processes.
  • Experience in welding and quality inspection of Carbon Steels, Low Temperature CS, SS, Duplex and Super Duplex Stainless steels, Alloy steels.
